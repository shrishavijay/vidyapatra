The Problem

Every year, millions of Indian students preparing for board exams struggle to find previous-year papers from their colleges for practice. These papers are often not available to students, as they're either scattered across the internet and not properly stored by colleges. Creating an information gap that disproportionately affects students without access to these PYQ's.

The Solution

VidyaPatra is a free, searchable database of previous-year question papers from respected colleges across Karnataka and beyond. Any student regardless of how limited their school's provided academic source material can search by subject, stream, or year and instantly access the same papers that well-connected students already share privately.

Who This Helps

Students preparing for PUC board exams in Science, Commerce, and Arts streams. Students in smaller towns without access to coaching centers that hoard these papers.

Built By

This platform was built end-to-end by students for students. As a demonstration that meaningful tools for educational equity can be built by anyone willing to learn.
